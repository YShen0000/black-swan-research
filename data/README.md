- This folder contains all the data you might need for running MVO. 
- They are categorized based on their functions.
- If you are doing the training, use All_SwanBeta100_MVO2 and optdata_MVO2.
- When you are running the code, you may download all the data, put them together and upload all of them to colab if that can save your time in picking the required files for every different run.
- `swans.csv` lists the 14 swan windows (benchmark, peak date, trough date). They were reverse-engineered from the Swan Beta files and reproduce them exactly; see `v2/swan_beta.py`.
- `prices/` holds frozen Yahoo daily prices (adjusted and nominal) from 1962 (or listing) to 2026-10. `SP100`: the S&P 100 Swan Beta tickers. `SP500`: the S&P 500 Swan Beta tickers plus every member since 1996 that Yahoo still has. Both include ^OEX, ^GSPC (price-only) and SPY, OEF (total return). Rebuild with `python data/prices/freeze_prices.py [SP100|SP500]`. Most members that were delisted before ~2015 are no longer on Yahoo. `dollar_volume_<universe>.csv.gz` holds daily close × volume (`freeze_volume.py`), the size proxy for the factor risk model. `tbill_13w.csv` is the 13-week T-bill yield (^IRX), the cash leg of the β-matched benchmark (`freeze_tbill.py`). `etf_close.csv.gz`: Treasuries (IEF, TLT, SHY), TIPS (TIP), commodities (DBC), gold (GLD) and the low-volatility ETFs USMV / SPLV, adjusted close (`freeze_etfs.py`).
- `universe/` holds point-in-time S&P 500 membership since 1996 and a sector table; see its README.
- `update_data.py` brings everything live use needs up to date (~4 min; run before each monthly rebalance). It appends the latest S&P500 prices and dollar volume (the new days are scaled onto the stored series, so old rows never change), fills the full history of any current member without stored prices, and refreshes the membership file, ETFs, T-bill and the sectors of stocks that traded in the past year. The SP100 and nominal files are research-only and are not updated.
